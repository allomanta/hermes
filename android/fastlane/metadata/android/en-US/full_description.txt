Hermes is an open, nonprofit and cute Matrix messenger app for Ubuntu Touch, Android and iOS.

Whether for your friends, your company or local community, Hermes supports a variety of features to cover all your daily needs.

Nonprofit
Hermes is donation funded.

Simply install, log in and start chatting. The intuitive user interface makes secure communication as easy as possible.

#### Secure

Easy
Hermes is made as simple to use as possible.

#### Decentral

Decentralized
There is no "Hermes server" you are forced to use. Use the server you find trustworthy or host your own.

### Feature rich and reliable

From public chats with tens of thousands of participants and polls to custom stickers, spaces, rich moderation tools, and multi-device support, Hermes delivers a comprehensive communication suite.

Hermes comes with a dream

Direct calls and video conferences, fully secured with Matrix RTC-compatible encryption.

#### Not another walled garden

We do not build another walled garden! Hermes is fully compatible with any other Matrix client like Element, Nheko, Cinny or NeoChat. Learn more at matrix.org.

#### Made by volunteers

Hermes is a non-profit project based on open standards and driven entirely by volunteers. Infrastructure is funded by donations, and participation is open to everyone.
